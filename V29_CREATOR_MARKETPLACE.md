# WHO SABI ME? v29 — Creator Marketplace

v29 expands the creator system into a marketplace/discovery layer.

## Added
- Creator storefront pages
- Creator following/unfollowing
- Marketplace search by title, creator and category
- Difficulty filters
- Creator categories on premium puzzle packs
- Verified creator indicators
- Creator follower counts
- Ratings and reviews for unlocked packs
- Recommendation endpoint based on follows, purchases, sales, views and ratings
- Creator discovery list
- Marketplace UI integrated into Creator Center

## Commercial note
The marketplace uses the existing virtual-coin MVP economy. Real-money checkout, creator withdrawals, KYC/AML, tax, refunds, chargebacks and payment-provider webhooks remain required before commercial launch.
