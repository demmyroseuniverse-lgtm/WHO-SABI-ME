# WHO SABI ME? v27 — Creator & Business Center

## Added
- Creator profiles with creator bio and verification request workflow.
- Premium puzzle-pack publishing with 5–50 words, difficulty and coin pricing.
- Creator marketplace for published packs.
- Coin-based pack purchases using the existing virtual economy.
- 80% creator coin share on pack sales; 20% retained by the platform economy in this MVP.
- Creator analytics: sales, creator coins earned and pack views.
- Pack archiving.

## Payment status
This version uses the existing virtual coin wallet. It does **not** connect real-money creator payouts or a payment gateway. Real-money purchases, KYC/creator verification, tax handling, refunds, chargebacks, fraud controls and creator withdrawals require a production payment/payout integration before launch.
